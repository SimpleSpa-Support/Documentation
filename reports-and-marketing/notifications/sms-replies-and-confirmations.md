# SMS Replies & Confirmations

When clients reply to a text or confirm an appointment, SimpleSpa shows it in one compact notification and in the SMS panel at the top of the screen.

## Notifications

New replies and confirmations arrive together in a single notification card instead of separate pop-ups. The card shows at a glance whether each message is a reply, a confirmation or a card payment.

From the notification you can:

* Mark a single reply as read.
* Mark all replies as read.
* Clear just the confirmations.

The **appointment confirmations received** alert clears once the confirmations are read.

## The SMS panel

Open the SMS icon in the top bar to see your messages.

* Client replies are shown first.
* Automatic appointment reminders are condensed into a single line.
* Messages are grouped by day.
* Use **Replies** to see only the messages that need an answer, or **All** to see everything.
