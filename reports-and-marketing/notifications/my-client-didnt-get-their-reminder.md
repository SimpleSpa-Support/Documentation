# My client didn't get their reminder

When a client says they never got a reminder, the cause is almost always one of the checks below. Work through them in order.

## 1. Check the client's contact details

Open the client's profile and check:

* **Email reminders** need an email address on file. A typo, such as `gmial.com`, means the email goes nowhere.
* **Text reminders** need a valid mobile number. Use the full number with its country code if the client has a number from another country. Landlines can't receive texts.

{% hint style="info" %}
In the appointment, the **Send Email** button only appears when the client has an email address. If it's missing, that's your answer.
{% endhint %}

## 2. Check the client hasn't turned reminders off

Reminders can be turned off for each client. Open the client's profile and make sure reminders are enabled.

## 3. Check when the appointment was booked

Automated reminders go out at the time you set (by default 24 hours before the appointment). If the appointment was booked **after** that point, for example a same-day or next-morning booking, the automatic reminder doesn't go out.

For those bookings:

* **Email:** open the appointment and click **Send Email**.
* **Text:** set the appointment status to **Confirmed**. This sends the client the text confirmation.

To change the reminder timing (24 hours, 48 hours or 1 week), go to **Settings → Locations → (your location) → Email Notifications**.

## 4. Check your SMS plan (text reminders only)

Text reminders need an active SMS plan on your account. Email reminders are free and always included.

* Not sure if you have SMS? See [How can I add SMS/Text Notifications to my account?](../../extras/faq/how-can-i-add-sms-text-notifications-to-my-account.md)
* SMS plans include a fair-use allowance per staff member each month.
* In some countries, texts can only be sent once a Sender ID is registered. See [Sender ID](sms-text-notifications.md#sender-id).

Texts are sent with a 5-minute delay, so a reminder for a just-changed appointment may arrive a few minutes later.

## 5. Ask the client to check their spam or junk folder

Some email providers file automated emails as spam or move them to a Promotions tab. Ask the client to search their inbox for your business name and mark the email as **Not spam**. Adding your location email to their contacts helps future reminders land in the inbox.

## 6. Check the message templates

If the reminder arrived but looked wrong or empty, review your templates in **Settings → Locations → (your location) → Email Notifications** and the SMS templates. Make sure tags are typed exactly, such as `[CLIENT_NAME]` and `[DATETIME]`, including the square brackets.

## Still not working?

Contact SimpleSpa support with:

* The client's name and the appointment date and time
* Whether it was an email or a text reminder
* When the appointment was booked

We can check the delivery log for that message.
