# Image Campaign

This page explains how to add an image to your campaign by email when you can't submit it through the marketing tools.

## Sending your image by email

If you can't submit an image campaign using the marketing tools, you can prepare the image yourself and email it to SimpleSpa support. Send the email immediately after you submit your campaign, and include:

* **Subject:** the subject of the campaign you submitted.
* **Media:** an image or PDF compressed in a web-optimized format (PNG/JPG/PDF), less than 5mb in size.

Once we receive your email, we'll add the media to your submitted campaign. You'll receive a quote for sending the campaign within 24-48h.

{% hint style="warning" %}
**Important:** Targeting is limited to clients who have not opted out of marketing and who have had a scheduled appointment in your SimpleSpa account. Marketing requests sent by email are immediately rejected.
{% endhint %}
