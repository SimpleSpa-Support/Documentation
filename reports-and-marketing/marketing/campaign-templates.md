# Campaign Templates

Templates fill in the subject, headline, message, offer, button and text message in one click. Then change the details to suit your business.

## Use a template

{% stepper %}
{% step %}
### Start a new campaign

Go to **Marketing** and create a new campaign.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Pick a template

Click **Start from a template** and choose one from the list below.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Make it yours

Replace the sample text, offer and dates. The **Before you send** check will remind you of anything you missed.
{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}

{% hint style="info" %}
Choosing a different template never overwrites text you've already typed yourself.
{% endhint %}

## Available templates

| Group                  | Templates                                                          |
| ---------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Bring clients back** | Win-back · Second visit · Last-minute openings                     |
| **Occasions**          | Birthday · Holidays · Gift cards · Seasonal special · Mother's Day |
| **Offers**             | Promotion · New service · Refer a friend · Packages                |
| **Updates**            | News · Hours update · Thank you                                    |

Every template's text message includes **Reply STOP to opt out**. Templates are currently available in English only.
