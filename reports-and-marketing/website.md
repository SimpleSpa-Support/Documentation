# Your Website

Every location with online booking turned on gets its own website at:

```
https://{your-slug}.simplespa.com/site
```

`{your-slug}` is the short name of your business in your booking link. Clients can learn about your business and book straight from the site, so it works well as a simple website or a link in your social media bio.

## Website Tools

The **Website Tools** tab in your location settings gives you everything you need to promote your booking page.

{% stepper %}
{% step %}
### Open your location settings

Go to **Settings → Locations**, choose your location and open the **Website Tools** tab.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Add booking to your own website

Copy the **booking button** code to add a **Book now** button to your existing website, or the **embed** code to show the booking page inside one of your pages. The button briefly reads **Copied** when it has worked.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Share or print a QR code

Click **Share / QR** next to **View your online booking page**. From the **Share booking page** window you can **Copy link**, **Download QR (PNG)** or **Print** a ready-made _Book Online_ sign.
{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}

{% hint style="warning" %}
If you see **Enable Online Booking first to generate the button code**, turn on online booking for the location, then come back to Website Tools.
{% endhint %}

{% hint style="success" %}
The QR code never expires, so you can safely print it on business cards, flyers and signs at your front desk.
{% endhint %}
