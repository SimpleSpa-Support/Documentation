# Accepting Deposits for Bookings

This page explains how online booking deposits work with Stripe.

{% hint style="info" %}
Online deposits are available exclusively with our Stripe integration.
{% endhint %}

## How deposits work

If you have enabled Stripe and connected it to your SimpleSpa account, you can accept online bookings with deposits of up to 100% of the value of the service, for single-service bookings.

* **Multiple-service bookings** are currently in beta and do not support deposits. However, a valid credit card is still required if you have deposits/require payment toggled on.
* **Deposits of less than 100%:** the client's credit card is stored securely on Stripe. You won't need to ask for their card again when you complete the sale, unless they pay with a different payment method.

## Apple Pay

SimpleSpa also supports Apple Pay for your business, so your clients can book online even faster and more securely.
