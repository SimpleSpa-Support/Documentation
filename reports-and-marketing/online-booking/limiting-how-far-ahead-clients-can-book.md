# Limiting How Far Ahead Clients Can Book

You can limit how far into the future clients can book online, for example no more than 90 days ahead. This helps when your schedule or prices change seasonally.

## How it works

* Dates beyond the window show as unavailable on the online booking calendar and date picker.
* By default there is no limit, so clients can book as far ahead as your staff schedules allow.

## Turn it on

{% hint style="info" %}
Contact SimpleSpa support with the location and the number of days you'd like, and we'll set the booking window for you.
{% endhint %}
