# Your Custom Booking URL

SimpleSpa lets you have your own unique booking URL. This page shows you how to set it up and explains the online booking options.

## Assigning a URL for your business

1. Go to **Settings → Locations → (your location) → Website Tools**.
2. Click the checkbox, and enter the URL you would like for your business.

{% hint style="info" %}
You need an active SimpleSpa plan to set up your online booking URL. Online booking is not available for trial accounts.
{% endhint %}

With online booking enabled, you also have the following options.

## Show Prices

Choose whether to show or hide your prices when clients book online.

## Show Available Slots

Choose whether to show only the available slots, or to show all slots. If you show all slots, the availability for a slot is displayed when the client clicks on it.

## Staff Selection

Choose whether your visitors select a staff member, or whether staff is assigned automatically.

## Gender Selection

Some businesses want their clients to choose whether a female or male staff member provides their service.

## Buffer

Choose from when clients are allowed to book (1h, 2h, 6h, 24h, 48h).

## Online Theme Color

Adjust the color of the top navigation bar on your unique SimpleSpa online booking address. We automatically adjust the text colors to match the color you select.
