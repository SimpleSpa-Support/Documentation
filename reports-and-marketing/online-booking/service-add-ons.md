# Service Add-Ons

Add-ons are optional extras that clients can add to a service while booking online, for example **Hot Stones** with a massage, a **French Tip** with a manicure or **LED Light Therapy** with a facial. They raise the value of each booking without adding more services to your menu.

An add-on can take time of its own, such as a 15-minute scalp treatment, or take no time at all, such as access to a relaxation lounge. See [Add-ons that take no time](service-add-ons.md#add-ons-that-take-no-time-0-minutes).

{% hint style="info" %}
Add-ons work on the [new online booking page](the-online-booking-page.md). Contact support if you're not sure which booking page your location uses.
{% endhint %}

## How clients see add-ons

1. The client picks a service as usual. Add-ons are not listed as stand-alone services.
2. An **Enhance your treatment** row appears under the chosen service, with a button for each add-on that goes with it. Each button shows the extra time (if any) and the price. The price is shown only if **Show Prices** is on for the location.
3. One tap adds the add-on. It appears in the booking summary, indented under its service.

Clients can also add add-ons from the booking summary after they have picked a date. If they already chose a time and the add-on takes time, they pick the time again, because the booking is now longer.

Removing a service from the booking also removes its add-ons.

## Setting up an add-on

1. Go to **Services** and add a new service, or open an existing one.
2. Set its name, price, duration and category as for any service. For an add-on that takes no time, set the duration to **0**.
3. Under **Online**, tick **Add-On** and make sure **Show Online** is ticked.
4. Save.

### Which services an add-on is offered with

* **Same category:** an add-on is offered with the services in its own category. For example, an add-on in **Massages** is offered with every massage.
* **Every service:** if you put add-ons in a category of their own that contains only add-ons, such as **Enhancements**, they are offered with every service.

{% hint style="warning" %}
As with any service, an add-on's category must be assigned to at least one active staff member on the **Staff** page. If no staff member has the category, the add-on is not shown online.
{% endhint %}

## How add-ons are booked

* **Right after the service.** An add-on that takes time is booked as its own appointment, straight after its service, so the full time is blocked on your calendar.
* **Same staff member.** The add-on is always booked with the staff member doing the service. If the client chose **Any staff**, SimpleSpa picks someone who can do both.
* **Checked before payment.** Before a deposit is taken, SimpleSpa checks that a staff member is free for the service and its add-ons. If not, no payment is taken and the client picks another time.
* **Prices, tax and deposits.** Add-on prices are included in the booking total, the tax and any online deposit.
* **Reports.** Add-ons show in your reports and the client's history as their own services.

## Add-ons that take no time (0 minutes)

Some extras don't need a staff member's time, for example access to a relaxation lounge, a glass of champagne or aromatherapy oils added to a treatment. Set the add-on's duration to **0** and it becomes an extra on the client's visit:

* **No extra time.** The booking is no longer than the service itself, and the times offered to clients don't change.
* **Booked with the service.** It starts at the same time as its service, with the same staff member, but blocks no time in anyone's schedule.
* **No staff check.** The staff member doing the service doesn't need the add-on's category. The category only needs one active staff member so the add-on appears online.
* **One confirmation email.** The client gets the confirmation for their service, with the add-on in their booking; there is no separate email for it.

{% hint style="info" %}
Because a 0-minute add-on blocks no time, it doesn't limit how many clients choose it at the same time. If you need to limit places, for example lounge seats, contact support.
{% endhint %}

## On your calendar and at checkout

* **Add-ons that take time** appear as their own appointment, right after the service.
* **0-minute add-ons** appear as a line inside the service's block, for example **+ Stillness Lounge: $15**, so they take no space on the calendar. Click the line to open the add-on's appointment, for example to confirm or cancel it. A cancelled add-on is shown crossed out.
* **At checkout**, the add-on is added to the cart with the service, at its own price, and both are marked paid together.

## Good to know

* Each add-on can be added once per booking.
* At locations that book two services at the same time, a booking that includes an add-on is booked one service after another instead.
* If you move a service on the calendar, move its 0-minute add-on to the same time too, or it is shown as a separate small appointment.
