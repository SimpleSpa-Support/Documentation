# Service Add-Ons

Add-ons are optional extras that clients can add to a service while booking online, for example **Hot Stones** with a massage, a **French Tip** with a manicure or **LED Light Therapy** with a facial. They raise the value of each booking without adding more services to your menu.

{% hint style="info" %}
Add-ons work on the [new online booking page](the-online-booking-page.md). Contact support if you're not sure which booking page your location uses.
{% endhint %}

## How clients see add-ons

1. The client picks a service as usual. Add-ons are not listed as stand-alone services.
2. An **Enhance your treatment** row appears under the chosen service, with a button for each add-on that goes with it. Each button shows the extra time and price. The price is shown only if **Show Prices** is on for the location.
3. One tap adds the add-on. It appears in the booking summary, indented under its service.

Clients can also add add-ons from the booking summary after they have picked a date. If they already chose a time, they pick it again, because the booking is now longer.

Removing a service from the booking also removes its add-ons.

## Setting up an add-on

1. Go to **Services** and add a new service, or open an existing one.
2. Set its name, price, duration and category as for any service.
3. Under **Online**, tick **Add-On** and make sure **Show Online** is ticked.
4. Save.

### Which services an add-on is offered with

* **Same category:** an add-on is offered with the services in its own category. For example, an add-on in **Massages** is offered with every massage.
* **Every service:** if you put add-ons in a category of their own that contains only add-ons, such as **Enhancements**, they are offered with every service.

{% hint style="warning" %}
As with any service, an add-on's category must be assigned to the staff who perform it on the **Staff** page. If no staff member has the category, the add-on is not shown online.
{% endhint %}

## How add-ons are booked

* **Right after the service.** Each add-on is booked as its own appointment, straight after its service, so the full time is blocked on your calendar.
* **Same staff member.** The add-on is always booked with the staff member doing the service. If the client chose **Any staff**, SimpleSpa picks someone who can do both.
* **Checked before payment.** Before a deposit is taken, SimpleSpa checks that a staff member is free for the service and its add-ons. If not, no payment is taken and the client picks another time.
* **Prices, tax and deposits.** Add-on prices are included in the booking total, the tax and any online deposit.
* **Reports.** Add-ons show in your reports and the client's history as their own services.

## Good to know

* Each add-on can be added once per booking.
* At locations that book two services at the same time, a booking that includes an add-on is booked one service after another instead.
* An add-on with a duration of 0 minutes is booked as 1 minute.
