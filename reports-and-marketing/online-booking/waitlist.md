# Waitlist

The Waitlist lets clients express interest in a specific service and time even when no appointments are available, so you don't lose opportunities when your schedule is fully booked.

***

## ✨ When Does the Waitlist Appear?

During online booking, if no availability is found for the selected service, staff and date, a **Join Waitlist** button appears. Clients can then enter their information and desired time.

***

## 🧾 Client Input

When joining the waitlist, clients are asked to provide:

* First name & last name
* Mobile phone
* Email address
* Desired time
* (Service and staff selection are passed automatically in the background)

## 📋 Selecting a Waitlist Entry

Click a waitlist row to open a detailed view, including:

* Full client details at the top
* Requested service, staff and time
* A real-time calendar view showing current availability

This helps your staff quickly find matching or close time slots.

***

## 🗓️ Booking From the Waitlist

Once you select a suitable time and staff slot, the system:

* Instantly converts the waitlist entry into a booked appointment
* Automatically notifies the client by email with their confirmed booking details

***

## ✅ Benefits

* Captures leads even when you're fully booked
* Reduces admin work for manual follow-ups
* Provides a seamless, efficient experience for both clients and staff
* Ensures no opportunity is missed
