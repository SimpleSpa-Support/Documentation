# My online booking is not showing availability although I am available in my calendar

Online booking availability depends on several settings. If any of them limits your time, clients won't see open slots.

## What affects availability

1. Staff hours available
2. Business location hours
3. Staff breaks and blocks
4. Holidays
5. Whether the selected service's duration is longer than the time actually available

## Troubleshoot availability

{% stepper %}
{% step %}
### Check the Agenda view

Go to the **Appointments** page and switch the view to **Agenda**. Check whether it shows availability there.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Check staff hours

If the Agenda view shows availability, go to the **Staff** page and check the hours for each available staff member: both their standard hours and their **Weekly Schedule/Roster**.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Check location hours and holidays

Finally, go to **Settings → Locations → (your location) → Location Hours**. Review the location hours and check whether any holidays are set up that affect your availability.
{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}

{% hint style="info" %}
If the selected service lasts longer than your available time, clients will not be able to schedule an appointment, and online booking will show that there is no availability.
{% endhint %}
