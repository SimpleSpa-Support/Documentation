# Managing your SimpleSpa subscription and billing

Everything about paying for your SimpleSpa subscription is in **Settings → Billing**. This page covers your own SimpleSpa plan. To take payments from your clients, see [Processing Credit Cards](../../daily-operations/pos/processing-credit-cards.md).

{% hint style="info" %}
Only account admins can see and change billing.
{% endhint %}

## Open Billing

Go to **Settings → Billing**. You'll see your plan and status, the payment methods on file, and your recent invoices.

## Add a payment method

{% stepper %}
{% step %}
### Click Add payment method

If you're still on your free trial, you'll also see an **Add payment method** button in the trial banner at the top of the page.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Choose how to pay

Pick a tab: **card**, or a **bank account** where it's available in your country. Every option your account supports is shown.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Enter the details and save

Card and bank details go straight to our payment processor, Stripe. SimpleSpa never stores your full card or account number.
{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}

### Paying by bank account

Paying from your bank account means no card expiry dates and no declines from card limits.

* **US:** ACH bank debit. You can link your bank by signing in to it, or enter your routing and account numbers.
* **Canada:** pre-authorized debit (PAD), using your transit, institution and account numbers.

When you add a bank account, you agree to let SimpleSpa debit it for your subscription. Bank payments take **2 to 3 business days** to clear, so an invoice paid by bank shows as processing until then.

## Your payment methods

Each saved method shows its brand icon (Visa, Mastercard, Amex and so on) or a **BANK** label, with the last four digits.

* **Make default:** the default method is the one we charge for your subscription.
* **Remove:** deletes a saved method. Add a new one first so your next payment doesn't fail.

## Invoices

The invoice list shows each charge with its date, amount, status and the card or bank account it was paid with. Failed charges are shown in red. Open an invoice to view or download it for your records.

## If a payment fails

A failed payment usually means an expired card, a card limit, or a bank refusing the debit.

1. Add a new card or bank account, or update the one on file.
2. Make it your default.
3. Contact SimpleSpa support if the failed invoice doesn't clear within a day, and we'll retry it.

{% hint style="warning" %}
Keep a working payment method on file. Accounts with unpaid invoices may be suspended, and reactivating an expired account can take longer.
{% endhint %}

## Change or cancel your plan

To upgrade, downgrade or cancel, click the plan button or **Cancel subscription** in Billing. This opens a short contact form, and our accounts team will make the change. Contact us at least one day before your billing date for the change to apply to the next cycle.
