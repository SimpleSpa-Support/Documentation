# Do I need to download updates to my computer?

No. All SimpleSpa updates are applied automatically, so you never need to download anything to your computer.

SimpleSpa was designed from the ground up so you can work from anywhere, at any time, without tedious manual updates. This means:

* The software is more secure.
* You always have the latest features.
* You can access SimpleSpa and all your appointments remotely, from anywhere, for free.
