# How can I add SMS/Text Notifications to my account?

If you are an active SimpleSpa subscriber, you can add an SMS plan in either of these ways:

* Visit our [Contact Page](https://www.simplespa.com/contact/) and select **Enable Text/SMS on my Account**.
* Simply email us.

We will adjust your billing and add the plan to your account. Please allow 24 hours for this feature to be enabled.
