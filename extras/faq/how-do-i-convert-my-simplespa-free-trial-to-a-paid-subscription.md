# How do I convert my SimpleSpa free trial to a paid subscription?

To convert your trial, add your credit card under **Settings → Billing**.

1. Open SimpleSpa and go to **Settings → Billing**.
2. Securely add your credit card to your account.

{% hint style="info" %}
Please allow 24 hours for the card to be associated with your account.
{% endhint %}

If your trial is ending, you will also see a **subscribe now** bar at the top of your navigation, where you can subscribe.

{% hint style="warning" %}
Account activations may take longer for expired accounts.
{% endhint %}
