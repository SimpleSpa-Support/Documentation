# Can I import my existing data to SimpleSpa?

SimpleSpa offers user-friendly templates. When properly filled out, this data can be added to a SimpleSpa account at no extra cost.

3. **Products:**  [**Download Excel File** ](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1uolHVSl-iPjntw9MK-z43nXQ_lHUxFDcXSYnXeuxUOo/export?format=xlsx)

For data in an existing system, first reference any documentation on that third party platform for exporting your data & then reach out for further assistance Our team will advise you on the best way to migrate your data

_\*Fees apply for importing Appointments, Gift Certificates, and Packages, with a quote provided based on your needs._
