# Can I import my existing data to SimpleSpa?

Yes. SimpleSpa offers easy-to-use templates, and when you fill them out correctly, we can add the data to your SimpleSpa account at no extra cost.

## Download the templates

1. **Clients:** [**Download Excel File**](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1OSiv35g4rKobJxiRtlIFLqZBAoka9KHALPysBm6A-x0/export?format=xlsx)
2. **Services:** [**Download Excel File**](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1lEBIRsfmU8yH8Za422lYqNyqITHVB26ZGz-QT0Cpldk/export?format=xlsx)
3. **Products:** [**Download Excel File**](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1uolHVSl-iPjntw9MK-z43nXQ_lHUxFDcXSYnXeuxUOo/export?format=xlsx)

## Moving from another system

If your data is in another system, first check that platform's documentation on how to export your data. Then reach out to us for further help. Our team will advise you on the best way to migrate your data.

{% hint style="info" %}
\*Fees apply for importing Appointments, Gift Certificates, and Packages. We provide a quote based on your needs.
{% endhint %}
