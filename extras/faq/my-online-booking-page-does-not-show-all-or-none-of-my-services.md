# My Online Booking Page does not show all or none of my services

If your online booking does not show your services, please make sure the following:

1. From the Staff page make sure that you have assigned to each staff the service categories that the staff performs
2. From the Staff page make sure that each staff available has the option "Show Online" checked
3. From the Services page, make sure that each service has the option "Show Online" checked

If you have done the above then your service category and services will show online during the booking process, remember that SimpleSpa will check if staff is available, shown online and also is assigned to the service selected
