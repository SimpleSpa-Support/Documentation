# Are the SimpleSpa Marketing Tools included in my plan?

Yes. SimpleSpa includes marketing tools for all subscribers.

## Filter and export your clients

You can quickly filter your clients based on certain criteria, then export the list as a CSV (which works in any spreadsheet program), a PDF or a printout.

{% hint style="info" %}
To export all your clients, first expand the viewable rows to the maximum allowed, then export.
{% endhint %}

## Send campaigns from SimpleSpa

Optionally, you can create your own SMS and email campaigns and send them directly from SimpleSpa. Based on the number of clients (recipients) you are targeting, we will quote you a price to send your campaign. The average turnaround for campaign approval is 48 hours.
