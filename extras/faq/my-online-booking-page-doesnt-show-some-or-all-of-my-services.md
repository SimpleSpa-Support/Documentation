# My online booking page doesn't show some or all of my services

If your online booking page doesn't show some or all of your services, check these settings:

1. On the **Staff** page, make sure each staff member is assigned the service categories they perform.
2. On the **Staff** page, make sure each available staff member has **Show Online** checked.
3. On the **Services** page, make sure each service has **Show Online** checked.

Once these are set, your service categories and services will show during online booking.

{% hint style="info" %}
SimpleSpa only shows a service when a staff member is available, shown online and assigned to that service.
{% endhint %}
