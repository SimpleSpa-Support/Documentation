# Does SimpleSpa have online booking?

Yes. You get a free SimpleSpa booking page, so you can go home at ease knowing your clients can book any time they want.

* **Website widget:** We give you a widget so you can easily link your booking page from your website.
* **Notifications:** When a new appointment is booked online, your client gets a notification and your business gets an email notification.
