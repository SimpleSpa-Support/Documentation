# Can I downgrade my plan?

Yes. To adjust your plan and billing information, contact our team before the end of your billing cycle.

{% hint style="warning" %}
**Please contact us at least 1 day before your billing cycle ends.**
{% endhint %}

We want SimpleSpa to always meet your business needs, whether you're expanding or downsizing.
