# I am trying to log in but it redirects to SimpleSpa.com

This usually means you have an invalid session in your browser. You can fix it by clearing your session:

1. Visit [this link](https://my.simplespa.com/logout.php) to clear out your session.
2. You will be redirected to log in again.

These steps should resolve any session-related issues you may be facing.
