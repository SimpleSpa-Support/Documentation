# Can I process Credit Cards with SimpleSpa?

Yes. SimpleSpa lets you process credit cards both in your store and online.

## In-store processing (USA and Canada)

In the USA and Canada, we offer traditional merchant services and can provide you with an EMV & NFC compliant wireless terminal for all your in-store processing needs.

{% hint style="info" %}
\*Terminal support is exclusively available for businesses boarded via our partner merchant services.
{% endhint %}

## Online processing with Stripe

You can also use a virtual terminal via [Stripe](https://stripe.com/global), with [Apple Pay](https://www.apple.com/apple-pay/) support for online booking deposits, in over 41 countries, including:

Australia, Austria, Belgium, Brazil preview, Bulgaria, Canada, Czech Republic, Cyprus, Denmark, Estonia, Finland, France, Germany, Greece, Hong Kong, Ireland, India, Italy, Japan, Latvia, Liechtenstein, Lithuania, Luxembourg, Netherlands, New Zealand, Norway, Malta, Malaysia, Mexico, Poland, Portugal, Romania, Singapore, Slovenia, Slovakia, Spain, Sweden, Switzerland, United Kingdom, United States
