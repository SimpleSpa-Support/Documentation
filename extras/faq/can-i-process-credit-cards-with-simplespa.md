# Can I process Credit Cards with SimpleSpa?

Absolutely, with SimpleSpa you can process Credit Cards; in the USA and Canada we offer traditional merchant services, and can provide you with a EMV & NFC Compliant Wireless Terminal for all your in store processing needs _(\*Terminal support is exclusively available for businesses boarded via our partner merchant services)_.\
\
With SimpleSpa you can also use a virtual terminal via [Stripe](https://stripe.com/global) with [Apple Pay](https://www.apple.com/apple-pay/) support for online booking deposits in over 41 countries including Australia, Austria, Belgium, Brazil preview, Bulgaria, Canada, Czech Republic, Cyprus, Denmark, Estonia, Finland, France, Germany, Greece, Hong Kong, Ireland, India, Italy, Japan, Latvia, Liechtenstein, Lithuania, Luxembourg, Netherlands, New Zealand, Norway, Malta, Malaysia, Mexico, Poland, Portugal, Romania, Singapore, Slovenia, Slovakia, Spain, Sweden, Switzerland, United Kingdom, United States
