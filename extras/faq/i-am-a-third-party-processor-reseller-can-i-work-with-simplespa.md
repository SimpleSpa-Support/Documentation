# I am a third party processor/reseller, can I work with SimpleSpa?



**For third party processors/resellers:**

## **Card Not Present**

SimpleSpa exclusively integrates with direct integrations that are certified by SimpleSpa. SimpleSpa does not partner with third-party processors or resellers for Card Not Present Transactions

## **Card Present**

SimpleSpa does not partner with third-party processors for Card Present transactions unless they are certified by SimpleSpa. We support boarding new merchants to the platform with supported certified integrations

**What happens to existing merchants who move to a third-party integration for Card Present Transactions?**

If existing merchants choose to switch to a third-party integration, they will lose direct integration support with SimpleSpa and can only process payments separately from the platform.
