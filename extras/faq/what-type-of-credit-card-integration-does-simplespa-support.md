# What type of credit card integration does SimpleSpa support?

SimpleSpa supports two integrations for credit card transactions: Card Not Present and Card Present.

## Card Not Present

With our Card Not Present (CNP) approach, you can securely accept credit card transactions.

* All the heavy lifting is handled directly by our CNP partners (Stripe).
* No card data touches your computer or your SimpleSpa account. It is sent directly to our CNP partners, which protects your customers' card information.
* All our CNP merchants need to complete an SAQ A and an Attestation of Compliance (AoC). For your convenience, these may be pre-filled and ready for submission, depending on the integration partner you are using.

## Card Present

SimpleSpa partners with the largest merchant services. Our unique integration lets you process payments with a physical terminal, so you can accept credit cards quickly and efficiently.

* You use one of our certified terminals with SimpleSpa to securely process credit card transactions.
* Our solution is semi-integrated: PCI compliance is not in scope, and the POS does not see the card data.
* The terminal handles all the card data and only passes back the authorization response.

{% hint style="info" %}
\*Card Present transactions are supported with certified terminals offered exclusively from our partner merchant services.
{% endhint %}
