# Does SimpleSpa send appointment reminders?

Yes. SimpleSpa automatically sends an appointment reminder to your client's email address 24 hours before their appointment.

You can customize each type of email reminder with your branding and choose what it shows. You can write it in any language, with your own custom wording (description) and instructions.
