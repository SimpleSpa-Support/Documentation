# Online Booking App

#### What is the Online Booking App?

The Online Booking App lets your clients install your booking page on their device like a regular app. It opens instantly and makes booking faster and easier.

***

#### Do I need to download anything from the App Store or Google Play?

No.

The app installs directly from your online booking link—no App Store or Play Store required.

***

#### How do I install it on my phone or computer?

**On iPhone / iPad (Safari)**

1. Open your online booking link in Safari
2. Tap the Share icon
3. Select Add to Home Screen
4. Tap Add

**On Android (Chrome)**

1. Open your online booking link in Chrome
2. Tap Add to Home Screen or Install App
3. Confirm the installation

**On Desktop (Chrome, Edge, Brave)**

1. Open your online booking link
2. Click the Install or + App icon in the address bar
3. Confirm to install

***

#### What happens after it’s installed?

* The app appears on the Home Screen or desktop
* It opens full-screen like a native app
* Clients can book without opening a browser each time

***

#### Will it update automatically?

Yes.

The app always stays up to date with your latest services, availability, and branding.

***

#### Does it work offline?

Booking requires an internet connection, but the app launches instantly and loads faster than a regular webpage.

***

#### Is this supported on all devices?

✔ iPhone & iPad (Safari)

✔ Android phones & tablets

✔ Mac & Windows (Chrome, Edge, Brave)

***
