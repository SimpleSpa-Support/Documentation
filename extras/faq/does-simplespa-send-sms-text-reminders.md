# Does SimpleSpa send SMS/Text reminders?

Yes. SimpleSpa can send an automated SMS/Text reminder 24 hours before a scheduled appointment.

* If an appointment is confirmed, another SMS/Text can be sent.
* Reminders can go to both your staff and your clients.
* You can customize the wording of the messages.

[Contact us](https://www.simplespa.com/contact/) to set this up on your SimpleSpa account. Your clients will love it, and you will reduce no-shows and keep your clients.
