# My staff will work a different schedule on a specific date. Can SimpleSpa handle this?

Yes. SimpleSpa lets you create a custom roster (schedule) for your staff for any selected week.

1. Go to the **Staff** page and select the staff member by clicking the **Edit** button.
2. In the **Edit Staff** prompt, go to the **Weekly Schedule/Roster** tab.
3. Set a custom weekly schedule for the week you need.

For more information, see [Weekly Schedules](../../daily-operations/staff/weekly-schedule-tab.md).
