# What kind of computer do I need?

SimpleSpa runs on any modern computer, desktop, laptop, tablet. SimpleSpa supports Mac and PC and can run on an iPad with ease. For optimal performance we recommend you use [Google Chrome](https://www.google.com/chrome/), [Mozilla Firefox](https://www.mozilla.org/en-US/firefox/new/) or [Apple's Safari](https://www.apple.com/safari/) as your browser.
