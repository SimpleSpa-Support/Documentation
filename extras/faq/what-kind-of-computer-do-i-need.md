# What kind of computer do I need?

SimpleSpa runs on any modern desktop, laptop or tablet. It supports both Mac and PC, and runs on an iPad with ease.

For the best performance, use one of these browsers:

* [Google Chrome](https://www.google.com/chrome/)
* [Mozilla Firefox](https://www.mozilla.org/en-US/firefox/new/)
* [Apple's Safari](https://www.apple.com/safari/)
