# How can I disable Credit Card Auto-fill in Chrome?

You can turn off credit card auto-fill in Chrome's settings.

{% stepper %}
{% step %}
### Open Chrome settings

Click the menu icon and go to **Settings**.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Open Payment Methods

Click **Payment Methods**.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Turn off auto-fill

Under **Payment Methods**, uncheck **Save and fill payment methods**.
{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}
