# What is Salon Software

**Salon software** is an essential tool for businesses and professionals who manage their salon's appointment scheduling and bookings. This category of software is also called appointment booking software or online booking software.

Salon software is a huge advantage when you run a busy hair salon, letting you easily manage daily scheduling, appointment bookings and inventory with a simple touch. Hair salon software lets customers conveniently book their appointments online, on the day and at the time they prefer, which improves the overall customer experience at your salon. With SimpleSpa's automated reminders by email and SMS, you can also reduce no-shows by as much as 70%.
