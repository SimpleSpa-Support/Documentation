# What is Microblading Software

**Microblading software** gives professionals a robust platform for managing their microblading appointments and bookings. This versatile software is also commonly called appointment booking software or online booking software.

Microblading software is an invaluable asset when you run a practice, making it easy to manage daily schedules, appointment bookings and inventory. With microblading software, customers can book their appointments online on any day of the week, at the time they prefer, which improves the overall customer experience at your business. Automated reminders, including email and SMS notifications, can substantially reduce no-shows by up to 70%. Intake and consent forms can also be sent to customers automatically when they schedule their appointments.

Your software should be tailored to the unique needs of your industry. That means recording before/after photos for client appointments, and using electronic forms, including customized Intake Forms and Consent Forms with customer signatures, all seamlessly integrated into the software. This keeps all the essential data for your business in a single, accessible location.
