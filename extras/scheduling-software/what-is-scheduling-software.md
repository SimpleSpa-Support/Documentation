# What is Scheduling Software

Appointment Scheduling Software, often referred to as appointment booking software and online booking software, empowers businesses and professionals to efficiently manage their appointment and booking schedules.

SimpleSpa is a web-based scheduling system, delivered as a Software as a Service (SaaS) via standard web browsers. The advantage of this delivery model lies in the seamless and immediate rollout of updates, encompassing continuous enhancements and the introduction of novel features. This ensures that your scheduling system remains at the cutting edge of functionality. Furthermore, the flexibility of our platform allows you to access your schedule and comprehensive business information from any location. Perhaps most notably, it provides your clientele with the convenience of online booking through their computers, tablets, or smartphones.
