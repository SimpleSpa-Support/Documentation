# Assign Tax Rate to all Products

On the main products page, clicking on the ![](../../.gitbook/assets/Percentage.webp) will bring up a prompt where a percentage can be entered which will be applied universally to the existing products, when new product is added there is an option to edit & adjust the tax rate per product
