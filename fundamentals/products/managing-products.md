# Managing Products

When adding a product, you can set the specifics of that product in the system including name, price, special price and tax rate along with notes besides other settings listed listed bellow

## Adding Product Categories

Before a service can be added, a service category is required so that the service can fall under it, press the Add Category ![](../../.gitbook/assets/Category.webp) button, and you will be able to create a category name, once the category has been added, you can then add a service within that category by navigating to that newly added category listed under

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot 2024-07-13 at 5.34.06 PM.png" alt=""><figcaption><p>Add Category button in the SimpleSpa Products Page</p></figcaption></figure>

## Editing Product Categories

To edit any product category, next to the Add Category button, press the ![](<../../.gitbook/assets/Down Arrow Button.webp>) button, this will bring up a popup to edit the name of the category

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot 2024-07-13 at 5.34.52 PM.png" alt=""><figcaption><p>The Category edit selection when pressing the dropdown button</p></figcaption></figure>

## Adding Products

Within the category press the respective ![](<../../.gitbook/assets/Product Button.webp>) for that category to add a service within that category

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot 2024-07-13 at 5.35.43 PM.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

## Adding Images

To assign an image for a product, click the product to edit it and then click the circular ![](<../../.gitbook/assets/Camera Button.webp>) Camera button to bring up the image selection/capture prompt to add an image for the specific product\\

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot 2024-07-13 at 5.35.56 PM.png" alt=""><figcaption><p>The Add Product button when hovering over the tag icon in the Add Product prompt</p></figcaption></figure>

## Editing Products

To edit a product, press the ![](<../../.gitbook/assets/Pencil Button 02.webp>) button, the new popup will allow you to adjust all the available fields per product selected
