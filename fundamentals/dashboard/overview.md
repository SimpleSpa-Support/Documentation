# Overview

The dashboard is an overview showing a collection of your appointments, transactions, receipts for a selected day. If your business accepts online appointments those are shown on the top, marked as 'online' appointments which you should confirm. On the left you will see Expected Appointment Arrivals On the right there you can quickly see Unconfirmed and Canceled appointments
