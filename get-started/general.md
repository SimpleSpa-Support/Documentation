# General

This page introduces the SimpleSpa layout and the setup wizard that helps you get started.

## Finding your way around

SimpleSpa keeps your key business information easy to reach:

* **Left menu:** Open business details, client and staff profiles, services, and settings.
* **Top navigation bar:** From the **Dashboard** or **Appointments** page, jump straight to appointments and the Point of Sale (POS).

## The setup wizard

When you sign up for SimpleSpa, a guided wizard helps you set up your account quickly:

1. Enter your business information.
2. Add your first services.
3. Add your first staff members.

When you finish, you can go to the **Appointments** page and make your first booking.
