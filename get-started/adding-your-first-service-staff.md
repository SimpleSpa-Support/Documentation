# Adding your first Service/Staff

Before you can book your first appointment, you need at least one service and one staff member in SimpleSpa.

## Adding a service

When you first sign up for SimpleSpa, a wizard prompts you to add a service. If you don't see the wizard, see [Managing Services](../daily-operations/services/managing-services.md) to add a service to your account.

If you go to the **Appointments** page before adding any services, you'll see the prompt below when you try to select an available time slot.

## Adding staff

After you add a service, you need at least one staff member (provider) before you can make your first booking on the **Appointments** page. See [Managing Staff](../daily-operations/staff/managing-staff.md) to add staff to your account.

If you go to the **Appointments** page without any staff, SimpleSpa prompts you to add a staff member before you can start.

{% hint style="info" %}
Staff need assigned service categories (specialties) before those services can be booked online. See [Assigning Specialties](../daily-operations/staff/assigning-specialties.md).
{% endhint %}
