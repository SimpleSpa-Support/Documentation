# Overview

The Dashboard shows your appointments, transactions, and receipts for a selected day.

## What you'll see

* **Online appointments (top):** If your business accepts online bookings, they appear at the top, marked as **online**. You should confirm them.
* **Expected Appointment Arrivals (left):** The appointments expected for the day.
* **Unconfirmed and Canceled appointments (right):** A quick view of appointments that still need confirmation or were canceled.
