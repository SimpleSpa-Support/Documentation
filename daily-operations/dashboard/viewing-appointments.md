# Viewing Appointments

This page explains how to view and work with appointments from the Dashboard.

## Opening an appointment

Every appointment for the selected date is clickable. Click an appointment to open a prompt where you can:

* View its details
* Edit the appointment
* View its transactions
* Process payment directly from the Dashboard

## Changing the date

You can switch the Dashboard to a different date:

1. On the left side of the Dashboard, click the date to open the calendar picker.
2. Select a new date. The Dashboard shows the appointments, transactions, and notifications for that date.
