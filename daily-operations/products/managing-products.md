# Managing Products

This page explains how to add and edit product categories, products and product images.

When you add a product, you can set its details, including name, price, special price, tax rate and notes, along with the other settings listed below.

## Adding Product Categories

Every product must belong to a category, so you need to create a category before you can add a product.

1. Press the **Add Category** ![](https://695085455-files.gitbook.io/~/files/v0/b/gitbook-x-prod.appspot.com/o/spaces%2Fa9tSKQ7Cexg8YEba5Evb%2Fuploads%2Fe1We57HvVaLZIaVlL74C%2FCategory.webp?alt=media\&token=aa27d35c-a41f-4c5b-a898-b0ef5908e456) button and enter a category name.
2. Once the category is added, it appears in your list. You can now add products to it (see [Adding Products](managing-products.md#adding-products) below).

<figure><img src="https://695085455-files.gitbook.io/~/files/v0/b/gitbook-x-prod.appspot.com/o/spaces%2Fa9tSKQ7Cexg8YEba5Evb%2Fuploads%2FRvluVOOgBbe28wiG9KdE%2FScreenshot%202024-07-13%20at%205.34.06%E2%80%AFPM.png?alt=media&#x26;token=030cd072-c8aa-44ac-9710-42dfe0ed83b2" alt=""><figcaption><p>Add Category button in the SimpleSpa Products page</p></figcaption></figure>
