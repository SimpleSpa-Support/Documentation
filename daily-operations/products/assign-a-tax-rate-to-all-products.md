# Assign a Tax Rate to All Products

This page explains how to apply one tax rate to all of your existing products at once.

1. On the main **Products** page, click the ![](https://695085455-files.gitbook.io/~/files/v0/b/gitbook-x-prod.appspot.com/o/spaces%2Fa9tSKQ7Cexg8YEba5Evb%2Fuploads%2FEjgI8HhdZhOJ0TzklG9y%2FPercentage.webp?alt=media\&token=64745f14-5abc-45d4-9548-47f077964e38) button.
2. In the prompt that opens, enter a percentage. It is applied to all existing products.

When you add a new product, you can still edit and adjust the tax rate for that product.
