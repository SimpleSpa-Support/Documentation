# Appointment Checkout

This page explains how to check out a client from their appointment and record the transaction.

## Checking out an appointment

1. Click the appointment on the calendar, then click the **PAY NOW** button in the lower right of the appointment popup.
2. The POS opens with all the services the client is scheduled for that day. Record the transaction.

When you finish, the appointment shows as **Paid**.
