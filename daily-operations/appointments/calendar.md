# Calendar

The SimpleSpa Calendar shows your entire business schedule, with all your appointments and all your staff, in a single window.

* **Colors:** An appointment's color changes with its status. For example, a completed appointment appears green, unless you change the color in your settings.
* **White slots** are free slots that you can book.
* **Grey slots** are non-working hours. Breaks appear in a lighter shade of the non-working color.
* **Drag and drop:** Drag an appointment to a different time slot, or to a different staff member's column to reassign it.
* **Click** any appointment to open it.

## Moving an Appointment When a Resource Is Busy

If you drag or resize an appointment to a time when its required resource (for example a room or machine) is already in use, SimpleSpa asks what to do:

* **OK** keeps your change, and the appointment is saved without a resource allocated.
* **Cancel** puts the appointment back where it was.

The new booking wizard asks the same question after you save a booking. Choosing not to keep it removes the booking.

## Live Updates Across Devices

When someone books or changes an appointment on another computer or tablet, your calendar updates on its own within about 20 seconds. You don't need to refresh the page.
