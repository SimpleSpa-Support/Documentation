# Apple Wallet

This page explains how clients can save gift certificates to Apple Wallet and how you scan them at checkout.

## SimpleSpa Gift Certificates can now be saved directly to Apple Wallet

* All gift certificates now include an **Add to Apple Wallet** button in the emails sent to the recipient.

### Why This Is Awesome

* **Convenient access:** Clients don't need to dig through their email.
* **Works offline:** No Wi-Fi is needed at redemption time.
* **Beautiful and professional:** Branded with your business's look.
* **Scan-to-redeem:** Works with QR scanning at the POS.

## The Gift Certificate Flow

1. 🎁 A client purchases or receives a gift certificate.
2. 📧 They receive a confirmation email with:
   * The gift code
   * A link to a branded preview page
   * An **Add to Apple Wallet** button
3. 🍏 They tap the button, preview the pass and add it to Wallet.
4. 🏪 When they visit your spa, they:
   * Open their Wallet
   * Show the pass QR code at checkout
5. 🎯 You scan the QR code using your POS or camera, and it fills in the code automatically.

## QR Code Scanning at Checkout

You can scan a gift certificate QR code using:

* 📷 A camera on your POS computer or tablet
* 🔘 The built-in **Scan with Camera** button
* 🎯 Your terminal scanner (if enabled)

The gift code is detected automatically and entered in the gift certificate field. No typing is required.

{% hint style="info" %}
The scanner supports both QR codes and barcodes, so if you add physical gift cards in the future, they will work too.
{% endhint %}
