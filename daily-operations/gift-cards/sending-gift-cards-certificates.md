# Sending Gift Cards/Certificates

This page explains how to print or email a gift card or gift certificate after you sell it.

After you sell a gift card or certificate, you can print it or email its unique number to your client.

1. Press the **Email** or **Print** button. A popup opens.
2. Enter the **FROM** and **TO** information.
3. Optionally, hide the value and hide the date of issue.
4. Send or print the gift certificate.

{% hint style="warning" %}
If you are emailing the certificate, make sure the recipient's email address is valid. After you send it, your client receives the redeemable card/certificate as a PDF attachment.
{% endhint %}

Both printed and emailed gift cards/certificates include a scannable barcode that matches the unique Gift ID that SimpleSpa generated automatically.
