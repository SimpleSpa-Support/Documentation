# Gift Cards

This section explains how to create, sell, send, redeem, reload and track gift cards and gift certificates in SimpleSpa.

## In this section

* [Gift Cards & Gift Certificates](gift-cards-and-gift-certificates.md): create gift certificates and set their fields.
* [Selling Gift Cards/Certificates](selling-gift-cards-certificates.md): sell a gift certificate in the POS.
* [Sending Gift Cards/Certificates](sending-gift-cards-certificates.md): print or email a gift certificate to your client.
* [Using Gift Cards/Certificates](using-gift-cards-certificates.md): redeem a gift certificate at checkout and extend expired ones.
* [Reloading Gift Cards/Certificates](reloading-gift-cards-certificates.md): add a new amount to an existing gift card.
* [Viewing Balance Gift Cards/Certificates](viewing-gift-card-certificate-balance.md): check a gift card's current balance.
* [Viewing History Gift Cards/Certificates](viewing-gift-card-certificate-history.md): see where a gift card was redeemed.
* [Apple Wallet](apple-wallet.md): let clients save gift certificates to Apple Wallet.
