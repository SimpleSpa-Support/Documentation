# Gift Cards & Gift Certificates

This page explains how to create gift certificates and what each field means.

SimpleSpa's gift card tool lets you issue gift cards that your clients can use with a rolling balance. Our integrated payments also support custom magnetic gift cards that can be scanned, with their balance always tracked in the system.

To create a gift card, go to the [Gift Certificates/Cards](https://my.simplespa.com/giftcertificates.php) section in SimpleSpa. If you haven't made a gift certificate yet, create one now. You can enter a name, a cost (what the client pays for the gift card) and a value (what the gift card is worth).

{% hint style="info" %}
We recommend creating one gift certificate to use as a template (with the same cost and value). Use this template when a client wants to buy a gift certificate for a custom amount. Create separate, unique gift certificates for any promotions.
{% endhint %}

## Creating a Gift Certificate

To create a gift certificate, go to the **Gift Certificates** page and press the **+Gift Certificate** button.

<figure><img src="https://695085455-files.gitbook.io/~/files/v0/b/gitbook-x-prod.appspot.com/o/spaces%2Fa9tSKQ7Cexg8YEba5Evb%2Fuploads%2Ft11FZXMCaetWewpAvwN5%2FScreenshot%202024-07-13%20at%205.47.48%E2%80%AFPM.png?alt=media&#x26;token=ab3bc8a9-0663-413d-815d-e741dfbfa7c5" alt=""><figcaption><p>The Add Gift Certificate button in the SimpleSpa Gift Certificates page</p></figcaption></figure>

Before you can sell a gift certificate in the SimpleSpa POS, you must create it on the **Gift Certificates** page and fill in the fields below.

### Name

The name of the gift certificate. It appears in the POS, online (if enabled) and in emails.

### Cost

The price the client pays for this gift certificate.

### Value

The amount the gift certificate can be redeemed for. For example, a cost of $50 and a value of $60 means the client pays $50 but receives $60 to redeem.

### Duration/Expires

How long the gift certificate stays valid, counted from the time of sale.

### Show Online

Display this gift certificate online (if Stripe is connected and your booking link is set).

### Can Customize

Allow your clients to change the value of the gift certificate when buying online.

### Image

To add an image, click the tag icon and select an image from your device. The image appears on the online page that shows your available gift certificates. **Show Online** must be turned on for the gift certificate to appear online.
