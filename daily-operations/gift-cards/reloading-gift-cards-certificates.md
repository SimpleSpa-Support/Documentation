# Reloading Gift Cards/Certificates

This page explains how to add a new amount to an existing gift card.

A gift card doesn't have to end once it has been used. With our integrated gift card system, you can enter or scan an existing gift card and add a new amount to its balance. This way, your gift cards can also work as a form of loyalty for your clients.

{% stepper %}
{% step %}
### Add a gift card to the sale

In the SimpleSpa POS, add a new gift card to a sale.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Enter the existing card and amount

Press the ![](https://695085455-files.gitbook.io/~/files/v0/b/gitbook-x-prod.appspot.com/o/spaces%2Fa9tSKQ7Cexg8YEba5Evb%2Fuploads%2FsVkYSv2n1ZIDDJCx5qWv%2FPencil%20Button%2002.webp?alt=media\&token=0519b972-51ea-4143-ab13-899b82280f8f) button. Enter the existing gift card number and the amount you want to add.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Update and complete the sale

Press **Update** and complete the sale. The gift card now shows a new balance that includes the added amount.
{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}
