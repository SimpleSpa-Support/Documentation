# Accepting Multiple Payments

This page shows you how to split one checkout across several payments, such as multiple cards.

## Record the cart on the client's balance

Check out your client as you normally would. Instead of using the buttons at the very bottom to save or process via your integrated terminal, press the small orange button just above them.

This closes out the cart with the total recorded on the client's balance. The normal receipt prompt then appears, with a quick collect payment button.

## Collect part of the balance

Press the **Set** button to set the amount to collect against the balance. For example, press **Set** and enter 10 to collect 10 of the 30 total.

## Repeat until the balance is paid

You can repeat this as many times as needed until there is no remaining balance. Most importantly, you can use a different payment method each time, such as a different card for each amount deducted from the balance.

For example, once the $10 above has been processed and the transaction saved, another prompt shows the remaining balance of $20. Set another amount and repeat the process as many times as needed.
