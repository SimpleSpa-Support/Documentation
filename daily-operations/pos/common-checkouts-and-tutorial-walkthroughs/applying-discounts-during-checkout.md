# Applying Discounts During Checkout

This page shows you how to apply and remove discounts in the POS cart.

## Applying a discount

1. Make sure you have added all items to the cart.
2. Press the **+Discount** button. A section opens where you can apply a preset discount or enter a custom percentage for the cart.

## Applying a nominal (fixed-amount) discount

Press the **% - $** button to switch from a percentage to a nominal amount. The number you enter is then applied as a fixed-amount discount.

<figure><img src="https://695085455-files.gitbook.io/~/files/v0/b/gitbook-x-prod.appspot.com/o/spaces%2Fa9tSKQ7Cexg8YEba5Evb%2Fuploads%2FpXuFw26AG0WeyIXpinpv%2Fimage.png?alt=media&#x26;token=f146bf80-f723-47e4-a3c0-cc251476b960" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

## Applying a saved discount profile

Discount profiles are applied to client profiles. To quickly apply a saved discount profile that is not on this client's profile, click the person icon. A list of all discount profiles appears, and the one you choose applies only to this cart.

## Removing a discount

To remove a discount, press the minus button on the discount line.
