# Using Gift Certificates in Checkout

This page shows you how to apply a gift certificate or gift card to a checkout and how to use only part of its balance.

## Applying a gift certificate

1. During checkout, make sure you have added all items to the cart.
2. Press the **+Gift Card** button to open the area where you enter the gift certificate or gift card code.

<figure><img src="https://695085455-files.gitbook.io/~/files/v0/b/gitbook-x-prod.appspot.com/o/spaces%2Fa9tSKQ7Cexg8YEba5Evb%2Fuploads%2Fxly8cq4Bxzs2XE1ppWB4%2Fimage.png?alt=media&#x26;token=45757ce4-19fd-470e-96ba-2d9b25235d32" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

3. Press **Apply**. If the gift certificate is valid, it is applied to the cart.

<figure><img src="https://695085455-files.gitbook.io/~/files/v0/b/gitbook-x-prod.appspot.com/o/spaces%2Fa9tSKQ7Cexg8YEba5Evb%2Fuploads%2FwIBDQSEIYPkHfWpK1Wk0%2Fimage.png?alt=media&#x26;token=707a7f8c-1507-44b5-9e36-797c9f0264db" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Once the Gift Cert/Certificate has been used during checkout the remaining balance will be used.

## Customizing the amount to use from a gift certificate

If you do not want to apply the entire amount to the cart, press the blue text that shows the amount being applied to the cart.

<figure><img src="https://695085455-files.gitbook.io/~/files/v0/b/gitbook-x-prod.appspot.com/o/spaces%2Fa9tSKQ7Cexg8YEba5Evb%2Fuploads%2FVs2MVloPEp0Cy74JQPoo%2Fimage.png?alt=media&#x26;token=6e76ae34-e61d-4ac1-b45c-5670aca5e52f" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

<figure><img src="https://695085455-files.gitbook.io/~/files/v0/b/gitbook-x-prod.appspot.com/o/spaces%2Fa9tSKQ7Cexg8YEba5Evb%2Fuploads%2FCt4HW2EcEtTnBZgU8SDn%2Fimage.png?alt=media&#x26;token=172c43cf-66ea-4327-abff-ad7439fde0c8" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

For example, if you set the amount to 10, it uses $10 of the $15, and the remaining $5 can be used at a future checkout.
