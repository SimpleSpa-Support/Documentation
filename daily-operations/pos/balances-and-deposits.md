# Balances & Deposits

This page explains how to record sales on a client's balance, apply credits and balances at checkout, and collect and find deposits.

## Balances

### Enabling balances

Go to **Settings → Locations → (your location) → Payments & Tax Rates** and add the payment option labeled **Balance**.

### Putting a cart on a client's balance

During checkout, select the **Balance** payment option. This puts the entire cart on the client's profile as a balance.

<figure><img src="https://695085455-files.gitbook.io/~/files/v0/b/gitbook-x-prod.appspot.com/o/spaces%2Fa9tSKQ7Cexg8YEba5Evb%2Fuploads%2FgtFfasR38J2l3lPYIeqL%2FScreenshot%202024-07-13%20at%2012.01.12%E2%80%AFPM.png?alt=media&#x26;token=d7454196-ee47-4e16-8750-a367795de729" alt=""><figcaption><p>The Balance payment option in the SimpleSpa POS</p></figcaption></figure>

### Finding client balances

Accessing client deposits can be done in two places. The first is the **Clients** page, which you can sort by the balance amount on each client's profile:

* Amounts greater than 0 are credits.
* Amounts less than 0 are balances.

## Deposits

### Online booking deposits

Deposits are designed for single-service bookings made online. To enable them, go to **Settings → Locations → (your location) → Website Tools**. Stripe must be connected. There you can set the option to require payment for online bookings and set the deposit amount as a percentage.

### Collecting a deposit in the POS

You can also capture deposits manually in the POS. This works best for single-service appointment bookings with a simple checkout. For example, a $10 deposit against a $20 receipt shows on both the receipt and the client's appointment.

{% hint style="warning" %}
Discounts cannot be applied when closing out a deposit. Tax is calculated during checkout.
{% endhint %}

### Finding deposits

There are two places to find deposits in the system.

#### The client's appointment

If a deposit was captured online or in the POS, it shows on the client's appointment.

#### The Dashboard

The lower right of the Dashboard lists all receipt deposits, with a total and a detailed list. From here you can open the receipt and close it out.

## Checking Out an Appointment With a Deposit

When you check out an appointment that has a deposit, the cart always reflects the appointment as it is now:

* If the service, price or staff member changed after the deposit was taken, checkout uses the current service and the remaining balance, so the amount due is always correct.
* If a client booked several appointments with separate deposits, every service appears at checkout.
* The final receipt always follows the appointment's current staff member, even if the deposit was taken under someone else.

### Deposit Under a Different Staff Member

If a deposit was taken under one staff member and the appointment has since moved to another, an amber **Deposit under different staff** banner appears when you open the sale.

{% stepper %}
{% step %}
### Review the deposit

Click **Review Receipt**. The deposit line opens with the appointment's current staff member already selected.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Save

Save the change to move the deposit to the current staff member.
{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}

{% hint style="info" %}
The banner never blocks the sale. You can dismiss it and continue checking out.
{% endhint %}
