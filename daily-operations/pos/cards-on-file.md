# Cards on File

Saved cards let you charge a client without them presenting a card, for example for a balance, a no-show fee or a membership.

## View a client's saved cards

Open the client and expand **Cards on file**. Each card shows its brand, last four digits and when it was added.

To remove a card, click remove next to it and confirm. Cards saved through older versions of SimpleSpa can be removed in the same way.

## Charge a saved card

At checkout, choose the saved card button for the card you want to charge. The charge always goes to the exact card shown on that button, even when the client has several cards saved.

## The card safety check

Before charging a saved card, SimpleSpa compares the client's email and phone with the details saved on the card's payment profile.

* Differences in capitals, spacing or country codes are ignored. For example, `+353 87…` and `087…` count as the same number.
* A warning only appears when both sides have contact details and they clearly don't match.
* If either side has no email or phone, no warning is shown.

{% hint style="warning" %}
If you see the warning, check you have the right client before continuing. It usually means the card was saved to a different client with a similar name.
{% endhint %}
