# Thermal Receipt

This page explains how to print a receipt formatted for a thermal receipt printer.

SimpleSpa is cloud-based. It prints to any printer, and works with any device, that is compatible with your computer and shows up as a printer in the print dialog.

When you make a sale or complete an appointment in the SimpleSpa POS, a print popup appears after the transaction succeeds. Click the ![](https://695085455-files.gitbook.io/~/files/v0/b/gitbook-x-prod.appspot.com/o/spaces%2Fa9tSKQ7Cexg8YEba5Evb%2Fuploads%2FRfTYSaTJDjrXUteYonDI%2FPrint%20Receipt.webp?alt=media\&token=77f7aa0d-c29a-421f-a6ea-37979e1545d9) button to print a receipt formatted for your receipt printer. All transactions appear on the printable receipt.

{% hint style="info" %}
While we can't guarantee that every receipt printer will work with SimpleSpa, we have customers who have connected other printers successfully!
{% endhint %}
