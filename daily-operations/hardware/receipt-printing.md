# Receipt Printing

This page explains how to print a receipt on a regular printer after a sale.

When you make a sale or complete an appointment in the SimpleSpa POS, a print popup appears after the transaction succeeds. Click the ![](https://695085455-files.gitbook.io/~/files/v0/b/gitbook-x-prod.appspot.com/o/spaces%2Fa9tSKQ7Cexg8YEba5Evb%2Fuploads%2FRfTYSaTJDjrXUteYonDI%2FPrint%20Receipt.webp?alt=media\&token=77f7aa0d-c29a-421f-a6ea-37979e1545d9) button to print on a regular printer. All transactions appear on the printed receipt.
