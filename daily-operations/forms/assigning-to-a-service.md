# Assigning to a Service

This page explains how to assign a form to the services that need it.

After you create a form, assign it to the services it belongs to. You can do this from the service itself or, for several services at once, from the form's **Links** tab.

## Links Tab

You can assign a form to multiple services at once from the **Forms** page. This is ideal when several services need to be assigned or unassigned a form.

1. On the **Forms** page, select the form you want to assign.
2. Click the **Links** tab.
3. Associate or disassociate the form with any service.

## Service

You can also add forms to a single service on the **Services** page:

1. Select the service and click the edit button.
2. On the right of the popup, find the **Forms** section. It shows all forms assigned to this service.
3. Click the **+Forms** button to add a form. You can assign as many forms as the service requires.

For example, if you created a consent form for Microblading, select the Microblading service and add both the Consent Form and your Pre-Treatment Questions for clients to fill out.
