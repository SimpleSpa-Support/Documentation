# Assigning Specialties

This page shows you how to assign specialties (service categories) to your staff.

Assigning staff specialties and service categories is a crucial step when you set up SimpleSpa. A staff member is only bookable online for a service if they have that service's category assigned.

## Assigning service categories to staff

1. Go to the [Staff](https://my.simplespa.com/staff.php) page.
2. Click the magic wand button ![](https://695085455-files.gitbook.io/~/files/v0/b/gitbook-x-prod.appspot.com/o/spaces%2Fa9tSKQ7Cexg8YEba5Evb%2Fuploads%2F44EQRE7mwsvoglj3TRz6%2FMagic%20Wand%20Button.webp?alt=media\&token=c3b3a08c-ae27-4656-9826-fa9f015f34d5) for the staff member.
3. In the popup, add or remove the service categories that the staff member provides.

## How specialties affect online booking

Adding or removing service categories/specialties changes the staff member's online availability:

* Staff assigned to a category are bookable online for the services in that category.
* Staff not assigned to a category are not bookable online for the services in that category.
