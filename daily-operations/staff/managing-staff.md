# Managing Staff

This page shows you how to add, edit and export your staff/providers.

To manage your staff, click **Staff** in the left menu. This opens the **Staff** page.

## Adding Staff

When you add a staff member, you can set their information in the system, including:

* Working slots
* Flat-rate commissions and hourly service rate
* Preferences, such as whether they receive appointment notifications, whether they are visible online, and whether their calendar is hidden on the **Appointments** page

Click the **Add Staff** ![](https://695085455-files.gitbook.io/~/files/v0/b/gitbook-x-prod.appspot.com/o/spaces%2Fa9tSKQ7Cexg8YEba5Evb%2Fuploads%2FRRY876oJpkTmCd43tloM%2FAdd%20Staff.webp?alt=media\&token=a69a1432-d40f-4ba1-b7c9-ae266d156240) button to set the general settings for this provider.

## Editing Staff

Once a staff member/provider appears in the list, click the pencil button ![](https://695085455-files.gitbook.io/~/files/v0/b/gitbook-x-prod.appspot.com/o/spaces%2Fa9tSKQ7Cexg8YEba5Evb%2Fuploads%2FV2Kz8ikPSV9jF39UMlBG%2FPencil%20Button.webp?alt=media\&token=3bfebd07-4b7f-4c4f-8d01-77b728a1fc8f) to edit them. You can edit any of the fields described in [Fields and Settings](fields-and-settings.md).

## Exporting Staff

To export your staff, click the dropdown next to the **+Staff** button.
