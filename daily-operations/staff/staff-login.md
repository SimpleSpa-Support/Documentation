# Staff Login

This page shows you how to give a staff member their own login so they can manage their schedule.

## Turning on Can Login

{% stepper %}
{% step %}
### Add an email

After the staff member has been added to your account, make sure an email is entered in their profile.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Turn on Can Login

Edit the staff member. Once they have an email, the **Can Login** option appears. Check it.

<figure><img src="https://695085455-files.gitbook.io/~/files/v0/b/gitbook-x-prod.appspot.com/o/spaces%2Fa9tSKQ7Cexg8YEba5Evb%2Fuploads%2FEb7iiS9kj44KEIieanJV%2FScreenshot%202024-06-24%20at%204.59.16%E2%80%AFPM.png?alt=media&#x26;token=adeb4e4d-716c-4abe-afbc-ab06c7745ec2" alt=""><figcaption><p>The login toggle on the Staff page</p></figcaption></figure>
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Save the profile

Save the staff profile. A welcome email goes out to the staff member, giving them access with their own username/password combination.
{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}

## Staff Welcome Email

The welcome email is sent the first time a staff member is set to log in. You can customize its wording to include your welcome text, instructions and any additional information your business requires.

To edit it, go to **Settings → Locations → (your location) → Email Notifications → Staff Email**.

Every welcome email includes your welcome text along with a password setup link, so the staff member can set up their account.

## Staff Access

{% hint style="info" %}
Once a staff member has set their password, only an administrator can edit it, from the **Users** page.
{% endhint %}
