# Adding Forms to Services

This page shows you how to attach one or more existing forms to a service.

1. Select the service you want to add a form to.
2. Click the ![](https://695085455-files.gitbook.io/~/files/v0/b/gitbook-x-prod.appspot.com/o/spaces%2Fa9tSKQ7Cexg8YEba5Evb%2Fuploads%2FOsbIAEV7X42uIO4tfDu4%2FForm%20Button.webp?alt=media\&token=8cc054f3-e2be-4d17-9b57-15bd33c047fd) button and add the form.

<figure><img src="https://695085455-files.gitbook.io/~/files/v0/b/gitbook-x-prod.appspot.com/o/spaces%2Fa9tSKQ7Cexg8YEba5Evb%2Fuploads%2FFW5DspuzhJchjPQUUGTE%2FScreenshot%202024-07-09%20at%205.34.25%E2%80%AFPM.png?alt=media&#x26;token=75695af3-0e42-4197-8901-23ca069cb837" alt=""><figcaption><p>The +Form button in the Edit Service prompt</p></figcaption></figure>

## What happens next

* Any appointments booked for that service now include the forms.
* If the service is booked online, its forms automatically show at the end of the online booking process.
* Emails can include forms using the \[FORMS] tag in the email reminder and online booking templates. See [sending forms](../forms/sending-a-form.md) for more details.
