# SimpleSpa Documentation

<figure><img src="https://695085455-files.gitbook.io/~/files/v0/b/gitbook-x-prod.appspot.com/o/spaces%2Fa9tSKQ7Cexg8YEba5Evb%2Fuploads%2FxZ5mgejVX6uZvkcLsgge%2Flogo_ss.png?alt=media&#x26;token=54053333-a0c3-410d-a7a5-68840cda4f95" alt="" width="286"><figcaption><p>"Keep it Simple with SimpleSpa"</p></figcaption></figure>

**SimpleSpa®** is scheduling and business management software for salons, spas and medical spas. It brings together online booking, appointments, a point of sale with card processing, clients, staff and commissions, packages and memberships, gift cards, forms, marketing, reports and text and email reminders.

{% hint style="success" %}
**New this month:** a redesigned Reports page with Forecast and Client At-Risk, a new campaign designer with templates, and export to Mailchimp. See [What's New](get-started/whats-new.md).
{% endhint %}
